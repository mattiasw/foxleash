Welcome to Foxleash

Foxleash lets an AI assistant on your computer, such as Claude Code, use this Firefox for you, on the sites you allow.

If you use Firefox from Flatpak, it also needs one setting before it can start the program. Type about:config in the address bar and press Enter, and accept the warning. Search for widget.use-xdg-desktop-portal.native-messaging (not the one ending in -proxy), click its edit button, change 0 to 2 and press Enter. When Firefox asks to start the WebExtension backend, click Allow.
